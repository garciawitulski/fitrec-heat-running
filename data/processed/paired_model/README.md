# Paired heart-rate model statistics

The three models use the same 18,626 sessions. The outcomes are mean heart rate during steady-speed segments and the mean across the whole session. All models adjust for runner effects, seasonal terms, prior recorded activity and fitness. One of the segment models additionally adjusts for a spline in running speed.

The builder removes runner effects and the other predictors from the two temperature spline terms and the outcome. `design.csv` contains the resulting two-by-two cross-product matrices. `outcomes.csv` contains the products with the outcome. Solving each pair of normal equations gives the two temperature coefficients. `joint_score_products.csv` contains the pooled CR1-adjusted score products for all three models. Multiplying by the inverse design matrices on both sides retains the covariance required to compare heart-rate measures in the same sessions.

`temperature_contrasts.csv` specifies the spline contrasts for P90 and P95 versus the median daily maximum. The steady-speed cohort with daily weather has at least 660 runners and is a subset of the 669 runners in the physiological cohort. Confidence intervals and two-sided p values are calculated at both endpoints of that range. The paired point estimates and standard errors use the full precision of the released matrices.
